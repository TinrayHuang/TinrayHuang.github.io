BEHIND THE WORK

The process matters.

Each project begins with a question and evolves through design decisions, prototypes, testing, and iteration. Here are the ideas behind the work.

01

Interactive Mechanical Eye Model

Designed to reproduce the motion of a human eye, this research prototype combines multiple rotational axes and axial movement into a compact mechanical assembly. Developed in collaboration with clinical researchers, the project explored the challenges of making complex biological motion tangible.

MECHANISM DESIGN · BIOMIMETICS · PROTOTYPING
02

Tree-Climbing Panda Robot

A bio-inspired robotic platform developed during my internship at the Industrial Technology Research Institute. My work included a redesigned gripping mechanism, moving jaw, integrated head system, and exterior structure—where mechanical performance meets character-driven design.

ROBOTICS · ANIMATRONICS · MECHANICAL INTEGRATION
03

Electrostatic Clutch Demonstrator

An ongoing research project at the University of Michigan, investigating how electrostatic attraction and friction can be used to control mechanical motion. The work connects experimental characterization with the design of a demonstrator that makes the technology easy to understand.
