I'm a mechanical engineer who loves bringing ideas into the physical world—especially when they move, respond, and feel a little more alive.

ENGINEER / DESIGNER / MAKERANN ARBOR, MICHIGAN

01 / MY STORY

More than
moving parts.

I've always been drawn to the space where engineering meets creativity. I love figuring out how things work, but what really excites me is how a mechanism can create an experience—something surprising, expressive, or simply fun.

My work has taken me from building an interactive mechanical eye to developing a tree-climbing panda robot and exploring electrostatic clutches. Different projects, but a common question: how can we make machines feel more alive?

Today, I'm pursuing my master's in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Michigan, continuing to explore robotics, mechanism design, and hands-on prototyping.
